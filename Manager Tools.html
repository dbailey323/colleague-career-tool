Holcim UK · For line managers

Your team.
Their next step.

Your colleagues own their development, and you play a big part in it. Three things make the biggest difference: identify their potential, develop it deliberately, and help them move.

Your role

Identify. Develop. Mobilise.

1IdentifyKnow your people and where they could go 2DevelopPlan development for each person 3MobiliseHelp people move on

Be known for growing people. It grows your career too.

Holding on to good people slows them down, and slows Holcim down. Managers who develop their people and help them move on build a reputation that travels. Talented people ask to work for them, their teams get stronger, and their own careers move faster.

What it looks likeYour people are put forward for roles across Holcim. Others ask to join your team. You always have someone ready to step up when you move on.
1

Identify

Start with a good career conversation. Then look across your team and beyond: who could step into which role, how ready are they, and who is missing?

Map people to roles

For each key role, including your own, name who could step into it. Include people outside your team.

2
Identify readiness

Place each person against the role.

Ready now1 to 3 years3 to 5 years
3
Check who is missing

Look at the list as a whole. If everyone has a similar background or path, or looks like you, ask why, and what you would do differently so everyone has a seat at the table.

  • Is “not ready” based on evidence, or on fewer chances to show it?
  • Who gets the stretch projects, the visibility and the introductions? Is it the same people each time?
  • Who have you not had a career conversation with yet?
  • Have you looked beyond the people you already know?
2

Develop

Most development happens on the job. Agree two or three goals with each person, and the development that will close the gap to their next role. Give the same support to everyone on your list.

3

Mobilise

Track progress, put people forward for roles and projects across Holcim, and have a successor ready so you can let people move on.

1
Track progress

Review development in your one to ones and update readiness as people grow.

2
Put people forward

For roles, projects and assignments across Holcim, not only in your team. Make sure the whole list gets the chance, not the usual names.

3
Be ready to let go

When someone is ready, help them move. With a successor in place, your team keeps going.
